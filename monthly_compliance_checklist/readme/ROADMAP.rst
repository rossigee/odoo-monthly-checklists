Known Issues / Roadmap
======================

Known Issues
------------

* The tier validation integration requires manual installation of the OCA base_tier_validation module
* Complex compliance conditions (electric/water bills) require their respective data modules
* RPC errors may occur with complex computed fields in certain Odoo configurations

Roadmap
-------

**Version 16.0.2.0.0** (Next Release):

* Bank statement import compliance tracking
* Bank statement notification compliance
* Email notifications for overdue items
* Compliance overview dashboard
* Export compliance reports to PDF/Excel

**Version 16.0.3.0.0** (Future):
